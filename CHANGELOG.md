## 1.0.0

### New Features

- **Roll EVs from your own sims** — Paste a Raidbots Droptimizer (a built-in link converter and a Show me how screenshot guide walk you through it) or import with one click from the WoWUtils addon. Sims are per spec, with a picker to import for any of your character's specs, and separate buckets for raid drops, the bonus roll track, and Mythic+.
- **Adventure Guide integration** — Coin markers on every raid boss and dungeon, two tagged value lines on each item (its drop value, and its bonus roll value plus drop share), tier tokens priced by what they convert into, an info bar with rolls available and planned bosses, and check marks on pieces you already own.
- **Weekly boss planning** — Click a boss's coin to plan your rolls for the week, per difficulty, as many bosses as you like. Mythic+ plans whole dungeons: one pool per dungeon. Planned targets glow gold.
- **Coin protection** — With a plan set, the roll button on every OTHER boss's prompt gets a lock cover (one click unlocks it), and the Pass button is guarded on your planned bosses. The addon never rolls or passes for you.
- **Bonus Roll Overview** — A full planning window: every raid boss or season dungeon with plan, rolled, and collected states, per-coin expected values, the best coin highlighted, and expandable gear lists with drop shares. /alp to open.
- **Drops Overview** — Raw drop pricing with no coin math: every boss or dungeon valued from your drops sim, with your Top 5 upgrades ranked in gold everywhere they appear.
- **Loot roll window values** — Need/greed roll windows get the Arc chest badge and the item's DPS value from your sim, priced for the instance you are standing in.
- **Bonus roll ledger** — Every coin you spend is recorded automatically: which boss, which difficulty, what you won, all browsable in the History tab.
- **Once-per-difficulty tracking** — The addon knows bonus rolls grant each item once per difficulty: collected pieces check off (scan your gear and transmog with one click), exhausted bosses auto-complete, and drop shares shrink as your pool empties.
- **Plan reminders** — A gentle popup when you open the Great Vault or receive a coin with no plan set (and a recap of your picks when you have one). Only for players who use planning; silence it per week with one click.
- **Item tooltip notes** — Items you won from a recorded bonus roll carry a note on their tooltip.
