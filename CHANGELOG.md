## 1.0.0

### New Features

- **Bonus roll ledger** — Every coin you spend is recorded automatically: which boss, which difficulty, what you won, all browsable in the Roll History tab.
- **Adventure Guide integration** — Coin markers on every raid boss, a bonus roll info bar (rolls available, planned bosses, roll counter), expected-gain and drop-share readouts on the loot list, and check marks on pieces you already own.
- **Weekly boss planning** — Click a boss's coin to plan your rolls for the week, per difficulty, as many bosses as you like. Planned bosses glow gold.
- **Coin protection** — With a plan set, the roll button on every OTHER boss's prompt gets a click-through lock (one click unlocks it), and the Pass button is guarded on your planned bosses. The addon never rolls or passes for you.
- **Roll EVs from your own sims** — Paste a Raidbots Droptimizer (a built-in link converter walks you through it) or import straight from the WoWUtils addon, and every boss and item shows its expected DPS value per coin. Per spec, with automatic re-import on spec swap.
- **Overview window** — A full Adventure Guide replacement: every boss with portraits, plan and done states, roll EVs, and an expandable gear list with drop odds and owned checks. /abr to open.
- **Once-per-difficulty tracking** — The addon knows Voidcores grant each item once per difficulty: collected pieces check off (scan your gear and transmog with one click), exhausted bosses auto-complete, and drop odds shrink as your pool empties.
- **Plan reminders** — A gentle popup when you open the Great Vault or receive a coin with no plan set (and a recap of your picks when you have one). Only for players who use planning; silence it per week with one click.
